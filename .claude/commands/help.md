## What is Executive Brain?

Your external executive function assistant. I help you capture, organize, prioritize, and schedule tasks in a way that works with your ADHD brain.

## Available Commands

| Command | When to Use |
|---------|-------------|
| `/morning` | Start your day. Review today's tasks, check overdue items, set top 3 priorities, time-block your day. |
| `/evening` | End your day. Celebrate wins, review incomplete tasks without judgment, plan tomorrow's priorities, brain dump. |
| `/intake` | Brain dump mode. Dump everything on your mind, I'll organize it into actionable tasks and add to TickTick. |
| `/quick-add [task]` | Fast capture. Give me a task, I'll figure out the project and priority and add it immediately. |
| `/stuck` | When you're overwhelmed or procrastinating. I'll help you find the next small action and get unstuck. |
| `/help` | Show this guide. |

## How I Work

1. **I break things down** - Tasks become 15-30 min chunks
2. **I know your energy** - Deep work scheduled for 11am-2pm (peak Vyvanse), routine stuff for afternoon
3. **I don't over-ask** - Smart defaults based on context, questions only when essential
4. **I'm not a taskmaster** - Supportive partner, not a critic
