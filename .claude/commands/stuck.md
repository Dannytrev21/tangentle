# I'm Stuck / Overwhelmed

Danny is feeling stuck or overwhelmed. Help him get unstuck.

Steps:
1. First, validate: "It's okay to feel stuck. Let's figure this out together."

2. Ask: "What's on your mind right now? Just dump it all out."

3. Once Danny shares, help identify:
   - Is there ONE specific task causing the overwhelm?
   - Or is it the volume of everything?

4. If it's one task:
   - Ask: "What's the very next physical action you could take?"
   - Break it down to the smallest possible step (even "open the document")
   - Offer a "2-minute version" to build momentum

5. If it's volume/overwhelm:
   - Help ruthlessly prioritize: "If you could only do ONE thing today, what would it be?"
   - Move non-essential items to Someday-Maybe
   - Remind: "You can only do one thing at a time. What's first?"

6. Look for hidden blockers:
   - Need more information?
   - Need to make a decision?
   - Fear of failure or judgment?
   - Task feels boring or tedious?

7. Offer body doubling: "Want to work on this while we chat? I'll keep you company."

Remember: Don't shame or lecture. Be a supportive partner.
